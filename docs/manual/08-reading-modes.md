# 8 · Reading modes

Eight modes read the same opened volume; switching between them never touches
the data. Each mode has its own guide, written from clinical reading practice:
not a feature list, but how a reader moves through a volume in that mode,
which controls serve which question, and where the mode stops.

| Chapter | Mode | The clinical question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| [8.1](../reading-modes/mpr.md) | MPR | What is here, in three dimensions: localize, follow across planes, measure in true mm, and present it. The default read; everything else is a specialization. |
| [8.2](../reading-modes/grid.md) | Grid | Show this whole region slice by slice at a glance, at a chosen spacing and any angle. |
| [8.3](../reading-modes/pano.md) | Pano | Survey the dental arch and jaws in one panoramic layout, then see any point in cross-section, including a traced mandibular canal. |
| [8.4](../reading-modes/tmj.md) | TMJ | What does each condyle look like in sections corrected to its own axis, and how do the sides compare? |
| [8.5](../reading-modes/reslice.md) | Reslice | Cut a fresh slice stack along any line or curve the fixed planes do not give. |
| [8.6](../reading-modes/ceph.md) | Ceph | An overall skeletal impression in a familiar projected radiographic format, reproducible off the same scan. |
| [8.7](../reading-modes/region.md) | Region | How large is this connected region by density, and where is it narrowest (the airway read)? |
| [8.8](../reading-modes/stitch.md) | Stitch | Align and merge two scans of the same subject into one volume when coverage spans both. |

> **Note.** Every mode is navigation and visualization. The software moves the
> camera, reformats the volume, and reports geometry and raw density numbers;
> segmentations and traces are reader-drawn, and reformats show only what
> their geometry samples. The authoritative read is the reader's, on their own
> screen.
